Price Distribution Analysis

Understanding where the majority of transactions occur and identifying luxury thresholds.

Top 10 Most Expensive Cities

Medina leads at $2.047M average, followed by Clyde Hill ($1.616M) and Yarrow Point ($1.195M).

Waterfront Premium Impact

Waterfront views act as massive value multipliers — modern waterfront homes reach $2.058M.

Historic vs Modern Homes

Historic homes (1900-1930) outperform modern builds in premium market segments.

Renovation ROI Analysis

Renovation adds significant value even at the same condition level — up to $712K for excellent condition.

Feature Comparison: Old vs Modern

Modern homes offer larger living areas (2,471 vs 1,837 sqft) and lot sizes at comparable prices.
